Three jobs, done in the screens you already use

Built for property accountants and bookkeepers who live in AppFolio's ledgers every day.

Export

Any ledger to Excel, in one click

Tenant ledgers, owner ledgers and transaction lists go straight to CSV or Excel, so you stop copying rows by hand for reconciliations and owner questions.

Review

A list of posting problems

Flags partial payments, NSF reversals and late fees posted out of order, duplicate or split charges, and fees on zero balances. You decide what to fix.

Close

Month-end status across properties

One checklist that shows which properties are reconciled, which have open flags, and what's left before statements go out.

Questions

Does Tallyhand change anything in AppFolio?

No. The first version is read-only. It reads the ledger pages you open and shows its findings beside them. Any fixes are made by you, in AppFolio, as usual.

Is this an official AppFolio product?

No. Tallyhand is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by AppFolio, Inc.

Where does tenant data go?

The checks run in your browser. Exports are saved to your own computer. We don't store tenant or owner data on our servers.

Which browsers does it support?

Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge at launch.

What about Buildium?

Not yet. If you use Buildium, say so in the form. Enough interest will decide whether it comes next.
